How to use this

Play the Civ V mod. When something new unlocks in the game, a popup appears with a short lecture and a button that opens the full page here — video, deeper reading, and sources. Grown-ups can also give the URL of any page to a kid's AI assistant (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) and ask it to quiz the kid on that page.
